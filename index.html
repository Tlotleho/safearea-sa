What to watch out for

    Where real data comes from

    • SAPS official crime statistics: quarterly, by police station
    • Crime Hub: open crime data and maps
    • SAPS missing persons notices
    SAPS publishes quarterly, not daily, so true “last 7 days” figures need a news or community-report feed.
